PaperReady

Get your paper ready to submit, for arXiv and ACL venues.

What it takes care of

  • Any zip: Overleaf downloads, macOS and Windows zips, nested folders, Latin-1 or UTF-16 files. The main file is found for you.
  • The bibliography: Overleaf's download has no .bbl; PaperReady runs BibTeX with your own .bib and .bst and adds it.
  • Review comments: delete or unwrap commands like \todo or your own \newcommand\rs[1]{…}; their definitions go too, and everything else stays.
  • Checks before you submit: missing and dropped files, the anonymous review version still switched on, stray documents arXiv could compile instead, and arXiv's 50 MB limit.
  • Faithful cleaning: the cleaning itself is Google's arxiv_latex_cleaner; PaperReady prepares the input and checks the output.

Tested on the official ACL, ICLR and CVPR templates and on the sources of published arXiv papers: the cleaned projects build to the same paper as the originals.
